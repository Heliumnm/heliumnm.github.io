# Liu He <span class="home-name-cn">何浏</span>

I am a researcher working at the intersection of **computational social science**, **speech and language processing**, and **explainable AI**. I received my Master's degree from the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.ustc.edu.cn) (USTC), advised by Prof. [Mengxiao Zhu](https://sites.google.com/site/mengxiaozhu/home?authuser=0) and Prof. [Jiahong Yuan](https://openreview.net/profile?id=%7EJiahong_Yuan1). I hold a B.E. in Materials Science & Engineering from the [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://en.hit.edu.cn) (HIT).

My work uses computational methods to study social phenomena, communication, and human health. I have also worked on large language models as an intern with [Baidu's ERNIE Bot team](https://research.baidu.com/Blog/index-view?id=183).

## Research

<div class="home-card-grid" markdown="1">

<article class="home-card" markdown="1">

### Computational Social Science

How communication modalities—text, audio, and video—shape public-health narratives and sentiment patterns on social media, with HPV-vaccine discourse as a central case study.

</article>

<article class="home-card" markdown="1">

### Speech & Language for Health

Computational analysis of speech patterns and linguistic biomarkers for the early detection and assessment of neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer's disease.

</article>

<article class="home-card" markdown="1">

### Explainable & Responsible AI

Explainable-AI methods for uncovering performance gaps and human or model bias in clinical diagnostic systems, supporting more robust and trustworthy healthcare AI.

</article>

<section id="projects" class="home-section" markdown="1">

## Selected Projects

<div class="project-list">

<article class="project-item" markdown="1">

<span class="home-tag">Speech & Health</span>

### Language Biomarkers of Alzheimer's Disease

Research on Mandarin speech biomarkers from sociolinguistic, patholinguistic, and AI perspectives. The work includes experimental design, a web-based data-collection system, a Mandarin speech corpus, sociolinguistic analysis, and explainable cross-lingual models.

[Data collection system](https://ustcphonetics.org)

</article>

<article class="project-item" markdown="1">

<span class="home-tag">Applied AI</span>

### AI-Powered Cognitive Impairment Screening

An applied research and system-development collaboration using large-scale multimodal data—including speech, text, linguistic-task performance, and lifestyle information—to support non-invasive cognitive-health screening.

</article>

<article class="project-item" markdown="1">

<span class="home-tag">Computational Social Science</span>

### Multimodal Communication of HPV Vaccine on Social Media

A cross-modality study of more than 1.5 TB of text, audio, and video data from Weibo, Douyin, and Bilibili, spanning 2010–2023. The project examines how the same public-health topic is framed differently across media formats.

[Project page](https://heliumnm.github.io/same-vaccine-diff-voices/) · [Master's thesis](https://github.com/Heliumnm/heliumnm.github.io/blob/master/_data/Liu_He_Master_s_Thesis_Written_in_Chinese.pdf)

</article>

<article class="project-item" markdown="1">

<span class="home-tag">Social Media Analysis</span>

### Misinformation Spreading on Social Media

Analysis of 110,000 Weibo posts and large-scale user behavior data, combining topic modeling with computational social-science approaches.

</article>
